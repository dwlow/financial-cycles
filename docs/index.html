IMF Advanced-Market overheating index calculated on live data for advanced markets.

For each economy we look at two metrics, equity and output. A metric is considered overheating if it's sitting above its long-run trend by 6.7% for equity and 1.3% for output. Readings are real-time and revised quarterly as data arrives. A complete explanation is available in the About section, and a complete explanation of the methodology is available in the Methodology section.

0 — Neither over the threshold 0.497 — equity over the threshold 0.503 — output over the threshold 1.0 — both over the threshold

Emerging-market cycle monitors

Exploratory credit/equity/property index for emerging markets with complete data. The index uses thresholds of +2.9%, +10.3%, and +7.5% and normalized weights of 0.242, 0.373, and 0.385, respectively. Filter bands are credit 5–43, equity 8–18, and property 4–29 quarters. Markets without all three required series are excluded; no partial index is calculated.

The signal each quarter

Each cell is what the index would have measured at each quarter. Dark underlines mark systemic banking crises as defined by Laeven–Valencia (2026 update, WP/26/94). Because the crisis window is capped at five years, a fading tail indicates that the episode may have continued beyond that limit, but after the cap it is no longer considered a formal crisis by the database. The database observes 1970–2025.

Method: Chen & Svirydzenka (2021), IMF WP/21/116. Filter: Christiano–Fitzgerald. Data: FRED, Eurostat, ONS, ABS, StatCan, IMF IFS.
